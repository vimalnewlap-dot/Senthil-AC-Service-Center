  • What types of AC systems do you service?
    We service ALL types of air conditioning systems including Window ACs, Split ACs, Cassette ACs, and Ducted/Central ACs. We work with all major brands like LG, Samsung, Voltas, Daikin, Blue Star, Carrier, Hitachi, O-General, Whirlpool, Godrej, and more.
  • Do you provide service for all brands of ACs?
    Yes, we service ALL brands without exception. Our technicians are trained to handle every make and model available in the Indian market, from older window units to the latest inverter split ACs.
  • How often should I service my AC?
    We recommend servicing your AC at least twice a year – once before summer and once after the monsoon season. Regular servicing improves cooling efficiency, reduces electricity bills, and extends the life of your unit.
  • Do you offer same-day service in Chennai?
    Yes, for most areas in Chennai including Manapakkam, Porur, Guindy, Tambaram, Saidapet, T.Nagar, Nungambakkam, Velachery, and Adyar, we offer same-day or next-day service. Emergency calls are prioritized.
  • Are your technicians certified and experienced?
    Absolutely! Our technicians are trained, certified, and have over a decade of combined experience in the HVAC industry. They undergo regular training to stay updated with the latest AC technologies.
  • What services do you offer at Senthil AC Service Center?
    We offer a complete range of AC services including installation, repair, general maintenance, gas charging, deep cleaning, water leakage repair, PCB repair, compressor repair, and also buying & selling of AC units. We are your one-stop AC solution provider.
  • How much does AC gas charging cost?
    The cost of gas charging depends on the type of refrigerant (R-22, R-32, or R-410A) and the amount required. We provide a transparent quote after checking the gas pressure. Contact us at 7200019872 for a free estimate.
  • Do you offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)?
    Yes, we offer flexible AMC plans for both residential and commercial clients. Our Gold Plan (single AC) and Platinum Plan (3+ ACs) include free services, priority dispatch, discounts on repairs, and extended warranty. Ask us for a custom quote!
  • Do you repair PCB and compressor issues?
    Yes, we specialize in PCB (electronic control board) repair and compressor repair. Instead of replacing the entire unit, we fix the faulty components at a much lower cost, saving you money while restoring your AC's performance.
  • Do you buy old or used ACs?
    Yes, we buy used, old, or even non-working ACs. We offer fair evaluation and instant cash payment. We also provide free pickup from your location in Chennai. Call 7200019872 to schedule a pickup.
  • How much does AC installation cost?
    Installation charges vary based on the type of AC (window/split/cassette), the complexity of the installation, and the location. We provide a free inspection and a clear quote before starting the work. No hidden charges – guaranteed!
  • How do I book a service appointment?
    Booking a service is easy! You can call us directly at 7200019872, send a WhatsApp message, or fill out the enquiry form on our website. We'll confirm your appointment within 15 minutes and schedule a technician visit at your convenience.
  • Do you provide warranty on your repairs?
    Yes, we provide a 30-day service warranty on labour and a brand warranty on replaced spare parts. If you face any issue with the repaired work within the warranty period, we will fix it at no extra charge.
  • Do you service commercial ACs for offices and businesses?
    Absolutely! We offer tailored AC solutions for offices, restaurants, shops, coworking spaces, and commercial buildings. We provide bulk service discounts and customized AMC plans for businesses with multiple AC units.
  • What areas in Chennai do you serve?
    We are based in Manapakkam and serve the entire Chennai metropolitan area including Porur, Guindy, Tambaram, Saidapet, T.Nagar, Nungambakkam, Velachery, Adyar, and surrounding neighborhoods. Contact us to check if we cover your location.
